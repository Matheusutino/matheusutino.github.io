**Area:** Large Language Models, Efficient Inference and Legal AI

Applied research project in partnership with the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS), focused on computationally efficient solutions for using large language models (LLMs) in the legal domain.

The work investigates strategies to reduce the computational cost of running and adapting LLMs, with an emphasis on efficient inference, quantization and knowledge distillation. Higher-capacity models act as teachers that generate supervision for smaller models, aiming to preserve the quality of answers and predictions while consuming fewer computational resources.

Activities include implementing inference pipelines, evaluating different models and execution settings, and running experiments to analyze the trade-offs between predictive performance, computational cost and efficiency.
