**Period:** 2025 – 2026  
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence, Multi-Agent Systems, Knowledge Graphs and Forecasting

Applied research project developed in partnership with OpsFactor, with support from CNPq, on using artificial intelligence to automate business operations, integrate systems and support decision-making.

One line of work was the development of an intelligent agent that interprets natural-language requests, retrieves information about the platform and executes operations through APIs. The solution combined language models, tool orchestration, knowledge graphs and GraphRAG to represent the relationships between entities and system features.

Another line investigated demand forecasting methods for multiple products, including statistical models, time-series foundation models and hierarchical reconciliation techniques. The work also considered evaluation metrics aligned with inventory management needs.
