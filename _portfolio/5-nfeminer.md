**Organizations:** SEFAZ-RS · USP  
**Period:** 2025  
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence, Data Mining and Natural Language Processing

Applied research project for mining and analyzing data from Brazilian electronic invoices (NF-e), aiming to structure, enrich and organize the product descriptions found in tax documents. The system combines natural language processing, large language models (LLMs), machine learning and clustering to identify similar products, standardize descriptions and extract structured information.

My work involved developing components for semantic enrichment, clustering and product identification, as well as implementing indexing and information retrieval with Elasticsearch. I also worked on integrating LLM-based agents for natural-language queries, developing APIs and containerizing the services.
