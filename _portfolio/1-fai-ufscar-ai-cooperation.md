**Period:** August 2026 – present  
**Area:** Generative AI, Process Automation and Document Management

Cooperation project with the Institutional Support Foundation (FAI) of the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar), aimed at training staff and developing artificial intelligence solutions for administrative and institutional processes.

My work involves researching and implementing AI agents, process automation and document analysis systems. Activities include integrating information from different institutional platforms, extracting and validating data from administrative documents, identifying inconsistencies in procurement processes, and building RAG-based information retrieval mechanisms.

I also develop solutions for asynchronous document processing using task queues and AI services, including the detection of possible duplicates among financial receipts. The work further covers the evaluation of language models, hybrid retrieval strategies and decision-support tools.
