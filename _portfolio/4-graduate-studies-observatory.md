**Period:** 2025 – 2026  
**Area:** Artificial Intelligence, Text Mining, Data Science and Public Policy

Applied research project for monitoring and analyzing the scientific output of Brazilian graduate programs, focused on identifying relationships between academic research and strategic themes of public interest. The initiative produces indicators that support the evaluation, planning and design of public policies for science, technology and innovation.

My work focuses on developing and evaluating machine learning and natural language processing methods to analyze the semantic affinity between scientific output and strategic themes. The approaches investigated include text embeddings, embedding fusion, supervised models, and graph and hypergraph neural networks.

The work includes experimental comparisons of different methods, analysis of results, and the search for computationally efficient alternatives for classification, thematic affinity detection and the interpretation of scientific data. It led to several publications, such as *Theme Affinity Prediction via Early Embedding Fusion Operators* and *Hypergraph-Based Affinity Degree Detection for Researchers and Government Strategic Themes* (BRACIS).
